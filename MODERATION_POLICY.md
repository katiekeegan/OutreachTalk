# Live Exercise Moderation Policy

The moderator is a human safety and relevance filter between audience submissions and the projected training dataset.

## Approve

Approve a submission when it is concise, relevant to the current prompt, appropriate for a mixed-age audience, and useful to the teaching goal.

## Reject

Reject submissions containing personal information, harassment, hate or sexual content, threats, graphic material, spam, attempts to disrupt the exercise, or content unrelated to the prompt.

## Status meanings

- `pending`: waiting for a decision;
- `approved`: included in the facilitator dataset;
- `rejected`: excluded from the facilitator dataset and removed from the active pending queue;
- `finalized`: exercise-level state that closes new submissions.

Finalization does not change the status of existing submissions. Moderators may continue resolving the pending queue after intake closes.
